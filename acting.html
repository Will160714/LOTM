On Acting

A potion is not drunk once. It is digested slowly, by living as the name on the bottle says you are.

I.Digestion

Every Beyonder carries a digestion value from 0% to 100% for the potion they last drank. When it reaches 100%, the mod announces “Digested the Potion”. There are two ways to raise it.

Acting deeds. Each pathway and Sequence has its own list of deeds, such as reading a written book as a Seer or killing in darkness as a Faceless. Performing a listed deed digests:

deed value × 0.007 × your Sequence number × (digestion_rate ÷ 10)

The digestion_rate game rule defaults to 15, a multiplier of ×1.5. Because the Sequence number is part of the product, a deed is worth nine times as much at Sequence 9 as the same deed at Sequence 1. Each pathway's Acting Methods page lists every deed with the digestion it gives under the default rules.

Using abilities. If no deeds are registered for your pathway and Sequence (as with every Twilight Giant and Black Emperor Sequence, and with every Sequence 0), using your pathway's abilities digests the potion instead. Longer cooldowns and lower-Sequence abilities give more. When deeds are registered, using abilities does not digest the potion at all.

A Beyonder who has switched pathways digests at half the normal rate.

Source: ActingHandler.java, EventActingTask.java, Ability.java, BeyonderData.digest

II.Acting Deeds

Each deed has its own cooldown. Repeating it before the cooldown ends gives nothing. The first time you perform a deed at a Sequence it is unlocked: the acting effect plays, and the deed counts toward restoring the acting cap (see III).

III.The Acting Cap

Advancing without acting leaves a Beyonder hollow. Every time you advance a Sequence, your maximum spirituality and maximum sanity drop by 15%.

The first three different deeds you perform at the new Sequence restore 7%, 5% and 3%. Together they cancel the penalty.

If you advance before finishing three deeds, the deeds you skipped at the old Sequence are recorded as missed. Performing one of them later still restores its share.

Death resets acting. All unlocked deeds are forgotten, and the 15% penalty is applied again for your current Sequence. You restore it by performing that Sequence's deeds again.

The cap does not apply at Sequence 0, in Creative mode, or when the applyNotActingPenalty game rule is off.

Source: ActingCapHelper.java, ActingHelper.onCopyPlayerData

IV.Sanity

Sanity runs from 100% down to 0%, and the acting cap lowers its maximum. It drains in three ordinary ways:

  • Exhaustion. Staying at or below 10% spirituality drains sanity after 5 seconds, and the drain grows until, after a full minute, you lose 10% every second.
  • Wounds. At Sequences 9–5, staying below half health drains sanity. The drain is stronger below 30% and 10% health, and stronger again at Sequences 9–8.
  • Overreaching. Casting an ability without enough spirituality costs sanity for the shortfall, at most 10% per cast.

Abilities that attack the mind, and some revival passives, also take sanity. Sanity damage is multiplied by your Sequence's resistance:

VI.Advancement

Drinking the next potion takes 5 seconds plus 3 seconds for each Sequence already passed. Whether you survive depends on your digestion and sanity. These are the rules on the mod's main branch:

  • Below 20% sanity, or two or more Sequences above your own: certain loss of control.
  • Digestion of at least 95% and sanity of at least 80%: no risk.
  • Otherwise the base risk is 65% (digested but unstable), 60% (stable but undigested) or 30% (neither). On top of that, the digestion shortfall adds up to 50% and the sanity shortfall adds half its size.
  • A Beyonder who has switched pathways before adds 10%.
  • A first potion is safe only at Sequence 9. Sequences 8–7 carry an 85% base risk, deeper Sequences are certain death, and low sanity adds to all of them.
  • From Sequence 5 or deeper, you can cross to the next Sequence of another pathway in the same Divine Group without risk, but only once.

Note: the hunger-games branch, which these files were transcribed from, relaxes these rules for its game mode. It ignores digestion when advancing, and first potions down to Sequence 5 are safe.

Source: AdvancementUtil.java (main and hunger-games branches)
